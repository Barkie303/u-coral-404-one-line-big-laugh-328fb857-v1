One funny line for the make.fun "One Line, Big Laugh" bounty — a single line of at most 200 characters that has to land entirely on its own.

> My screen time report said 6 hours yesterday. I opened it on my phone to check. It's 7 now.

`main.ts` writes that line to `entry.txt` in the output directory.
